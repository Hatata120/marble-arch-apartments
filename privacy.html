Privacy

Privacy notice

How Marble Arch Apartments Ltd uses the details you share with us through this website.

Who we are

Marble Arch Apartments Ltd is registered in england and wales with company number 16857545. Our registered office is Flat 1 to 4, 182 to 184 Edgware Road, London W2 2DS.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we receive the details you type into the form: your name, email address, telephone number if you add it, and what you tell us about your stay or your apartment.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to arrange your stay or our work on your apartment. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a stay or an agreement are deleted within twelve months.

Bookings on other platforms

When you book on Londoners.com, Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Expedia or Plum Guide, that platform's own privacy notice also applies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Contact us at info@marble-arch-apartments.com.
