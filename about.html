About us

We prepare apartments and look after them, from A to Z

Marble Arch Apartments was founded in London in 2025 to do one thing well: take good apartments in Central London, prepare them to the standard of a fine hotel, and look after them and their guests as if they were our own.

What we do

Two sides of the same care

For guests, we run four serviced apartments in one building at Marble Arch, with three bedrooms in most of them, a lift to every door and the details that make a stay easy: self check in, fast wifi, air conditioning, toilet bidets and a team that answers.

For owners, we take apartments in Central London and handle everything: the preparation, the listing on six platforms, the guests, the housekeeping, the repairs and the compliance. The owner hands us the keys.

How we work

Three things we hold to

Care

Every apartment is checked before every arrival. Fresh linen, full coffee, working wifi, and a note if something has changed.

Precision

Times kept to the minute, prices shown in full, and every promise on a listing true on arrival.

Discretion

The address goes to the guest once, with the arrival details. Owners and guests are never named without permission.

Why Marble Arch

The corner where London opens up

Hyde Park, Oxford Street, Mayfair and Marylebone meet at the Arch. Paddington brings the Elizabeth line and the Heathrow Express within a short walk. It is the address we would choose for ourselves.
