Apartment II

Quintessentially British

Two king bedrooms and a London classic, freshly done.

  • 2 bedrooms
  • 1.5 bathrooms
  • Sleeps 6

The apartment

Two king bedrooms, a bathroom and a separate cloakroom, finished with vintage London prints, black leather sofas and warm oak.

The living room gathers around black leather sofas, marble and brass coffee tables and a 75 inch Smart TV set into an oak panel wall. The dining table, topped in black marble, seats four.

Both bedrooms have king beds, tall mirrors and dressing tables, and the living room sofa opens into a queen bed for a third and fourth guest.

The bathroom has a bath, travertine tiles and a lit mirror, with a toilet bidet in the bathroom and the cloakroom. Air conditioning is fixed throughout.
